Privacy Policy


Effective Date: July 11, 2026



1. Introduction


This Privacy Policy explains how BiVi handles information in connection with your use of the BiVi AI chatbot platform. It applies wherever you access BiVi, including through Discord, WhatsApp (via the WhatsApp Cloud API), Instagram, and Google Chat.


This Policy works alongside our Terms of Use. If there is ever a conflict between the two on a privacy-related matter, this Policy governs.




2. Our Approach to This Policy


BiVi is an actively developing platform, and the way we handle information will naturally grow as new features are added. Rather than rewriting this Policy every time a small detail changes, we have written it to describe both what BiVi does today and what BiVi is expected to do as it develops, so this Policy stays accurate over a longer period of time.


Because of this, some parts of this Policy describe planned future practices rather than something that is already happening. We have clearly separated "current practice" from "planned practice" throughout this document so there is no confusion about what applies right now.


Adding new fields within a category that this Policy already describes — for example, a new profile detail alongside the account information already listed in Section 3 — does not require us to publish a new version of this Policy. However, if BiVi ever begins collecting a genuinely new category of information not already described here, or starts using information for a materially different purpose, we will update this Policy and revise the effective date accordingly, consistent with Section 11.




3. Information We Collect


3.1 Current Practice: No Persistent Database


As of the effective date of this Policy, BiVi does not operate a persistent database and does not store your account details, profile information, or chat history on BiVi's own servers. BiVi is currently "databaseless," meaning information is processed only for as long as it takes to generate a response to you.


3.2 Information Processed to Generate a Response


When you send a message to BiVi, the content of that message is processed in order to generate a reply. This may include passing your message to one or more AI providers described in Section 5. Under BiVi's current, databaseless setup, this message content is not saved by BiVi after a response has been generated and delivered to you.


3.3 Planned Future Collection: Account Information


As BiVi grows, we plan to introduce a database layer to support account-related features, such as recognizing you across sessions and platforms. Once introduced, this database is expected to store account-identifying information such as:



  • A unique user ID assigned by BiVi

  • Your name and username

  • Your WhatsApp number, where you use BiVi through WhatsApp

  • Your Instagram ID, where you use BiVi through Instagram

  • Your Discord username and associated email address, where you use BiVi through Discord

  • Your public profile picture, where made available by the platform you use

This planned database is intended to store account-identifying information only. It is not designed to store your chat or message history. If this scope ever changes so that BiVi begins storing message content or chat history, we will treat that as a material change and will update this Policy and its effective date, as described in Section 11, rather than relying on this section alone.




4. How We Use Information


Any information BiVi processes, now or once the planned database is introduced, is used only for purposes connected to operating and improving BiVi, such as:



  • Generating a response to the message you send

  • Recognizing your account across sessions, once account features are introduced

  • Displaying basic profile information back to you within the chat experience

  • Maintaining the security and reliability of BiVi

  • Responding to support requests you send us

  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not use account information described in Section 3.3 to build a profile of your conversations, since that information is separate from, and does not include, any chat or message history.




5. How Information Is Shared


BiVi shares information only where necessary to operate the platform:



  • AI providers. Your message content is shared with the AI provider or providers used to generate a response to that message.

  • Messaging platforms. Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Google Chat each process information as part of delivering your messages to and from BiVi, under their own privacy policies.

  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information where required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of BiVi, our users, or others.

BiVi does not sell your personal information.




6. Data Retention


Under BiVi's current, databaseless setup, there is no chat history or account record for BiVi to retain, since message content is not saved after a response is delivered.


Once the planned account database described in Section 3.3 is introduced, account information will be kept for as long as your account remains active, or as long as needed to provide BiVi's services to you. You may request deletion of your account information at any time using the contact details in Section 12.




7. Your Rights and Choices


Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding the information BiVi holds about you, which may include the right to:



  • Ask what account information, if any, BiVi holds about you

  • Ask us to correct inaccurate account information

  • Ask us to delete your account information

  • Object to certain uses of your information, where applicable under your local law

You can exercise these rights by contacting us using the details in Section 12. You can also stop using BiVi at any time, as described in our Terms of Use.




8. Children's Privacy


BiVi is not intended for use by anyone below the minimum age described in our Terms of Use. We do not knowingly collect account information from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with account information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can address it.




9. Security


We take reasonable steps intended to protect information connected to BiVi from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without authorization.




10. International Users


BiVi is available globally, and information may be processed in countries other than the one you live in, including countries where our AI providers or the messaging platforms we rely on operate their systems. By using BiVi, you understand that your information may be processed outside your home country.




11. Changes to This Policy


We may update this Policy as BiVi's features and practices develop. Minor additions within a category already described in this Policy, such as an extra account field, do not require a new version. Where we introduce a genuinely new category of information collected, or a materially different use of information, we will revise this Policy, update the effective date at the top of this page, and, where appropriate, take reasonable steps to bring the change to your attention.


Your continued use of BiVi after an updated Policy has been posted means that you accept the updated Policy.
